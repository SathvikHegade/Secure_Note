Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SecureNote

How secure are my notes?
Your notes are protected with bcrypt password hashing (10 rounds). Private notes require a password to access, and all data is securely stored in PostgreSQL. Only you have access to your notes with the correct URL and password combination.
What's the difference between public and private notes?
Private notes require a password and never expire. Public notes don't require a password but auto-delete after your chosen time period (1 hour to 7 days). Choose public for temporary sharing and private for long-term storage.
How does auto-save work?
Your changes are automatically saved 2 seconds after you stop typing. You'll see a "Saved" indicator when your content is safely stored. No need to manually save!
What file types can I upload?
You can upload PDF, JPG, PNG, and DOCX files up to 10MB each. Files are stored securely on Cloudinary CDN for fast access. File expiration follows your note's retention settings.
How does AI summarization work?
Click the "Summarize" button to generate an AI-powered summary using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash. It analyzes your note content and provides key insights, statistics, and a concise overview.

About SecureNote

Features

  • Custom URLs: Create memorable, unique URLs for your notepads
  • Password Protection: bcrypt-hashed passwords keep your notes secure
  • Auto-Save: Changes are saved automatically every few seconds
  • AI Summarization: Get concise summaries powered by Google Gemini
  • File Uploads: Attach PDFs, images, and documents (max 10MB)
  • Dark Mode: Toggle between light and dark themes

Privacy & Security

Your notes are stored securely with bcrypt password hashing (10 rounds). Only you can access your note with the correct URL and password combination.

File Management

Private notes: Files persist indefinitely without expiration.
Public notes: Files auto-delete after your chosen retention period (1 hour to 7 days).
Supported formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX (max 10MB per file).
